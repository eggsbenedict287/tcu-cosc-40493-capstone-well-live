# Well Live education-first app mockups

These mockups explore a mobile-first Well Live experience in which reviewed health education receives the primary navigation and visual emphasis, while community support remains an important but clearly separated part of the product.

## View the mockups

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser. The gallery links to ten full phone-sized screens and does not require a build step or internet connection.

## Content balance

- Six education screens: Learning Feed, Explore Topics, Evidence Article, Video Lesson, Learning Path, and Source-Backed Guide.
- Four community screens: Community Feed, Topic Group, Share Experience, and Chat Room.

The education screens demonstrate source attribution, named reviewers, review and update dates, accessible media options, transparent personalization, and general-education disclaimers. The community screens label user content as lived experience, keep anonymous participation available, and place reviewed resources near discussions.

## Files

- `index.html`: comparison gallery for all ten screens.
- `app.html`: the shared phone mockup surface selected through the `screen` query parameter.
- `app.css`: responsive layout and visual styling.
- `app.js`: screen switching and small prototype interactions.

## Prototype status

This is a design prototype, not production application code. Names, health content, sources, dates, and interactions are representative placeholders for requirements and client review.
